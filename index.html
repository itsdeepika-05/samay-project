How the Service Guarantee Clock Works

Under the Public Service Guarantee Act (RTS), statutory service commitments become predictable workflows.

Statutory 15-day service model
✓
Day 0 Application Submitted Receipt issued, tracking reference generated, statutory clock starts.
✓
Day 1–10 Verification & Processing Assigned desk scrutinises documents; progress visibility provided.
!
Day 10–15 Due Soon Alert Window High-priority alert to citizen and supervisor to prevent breach.
⚖
Day 15+ Statutory Breach & Appeal Citizen is legally empowered to file a First Appeal with automated grounds.
