LEGAL EMPOWERMENT

Your Rights Under the Right to Public Services Act

The Right to Public Services (RTS) legislation transforms administrative services from discretionary favors into legally enforceable citizen rights.

⚖
Right to a Time-Bound Decision

Every citizen who applies with required documents is legally guaranteed disposal within the notified timeframe.

📋
Mandatory Reason for Rejection

No desk can arbitrarily reject an application without documenting specific legal grounds in writing.

⚡
First Appeal Entitlement (Section 8)

If the delivery date expires without disposal, citizens hold an uninhibited right to file a First Appeal to senior authorities.

🛡
Officer Penalty Clause

Appellate authorities have the statutory power to deduct compensatory penalties from defaulting officers' salaries.

🏛
